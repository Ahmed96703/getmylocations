How to check the age of a domain or website

  • Enter the domain, such as example.com, or paste the full website address. Subdomains and page paths are removed for you.
  • Press Check age. The checker asks the domain’s official registry for its record, the same data a WHOIS domain age lookup shows.
  • Read the result: the domain age in years, months and days, the domain registration date (the domain creation date), the expiry date with time left, the registrar and the name servers.

This domain age checker works as a domain age calculator: it counts from the registry’s creation date to today, so the figure is exact to the day, not rounded to the year.

Why check a domain’s age

  • Spotting scams: a shop or bank site registered a few weeks ago deserves extra caution. Phishing sites are often registered only days before they are used.
  • Buying a domain: check its domain history and expiry before paying.
  • SEO and link building: see how long a site has been around before you buy a link, a guest post or the whole site.
  • Your own domains: use it as a domain expiry date checker and see the expiry date before it lapses and someone else takes it.

For most purposes, website age and domain age are close, so this website age checker answers both “how old is a domain” and “how old is a website”. If you need to know what was on a domain before, open its earliest snapshot on the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine.

Reading the domain status

  • client transfer prohibited: transfer lock set by the registrar, normal and recommended.
  • active / ok: no restrictions, the domain works normally.
  • redemption period / pending delete: the domain has expired and will soon be released.
  • server hold / client hold: the domain is suspended and will not resolve.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check domain age?

Type the domain or paste the website’s address into the checker and press Check age. It reads the registration date from the registry that runs the domain’s ending (such as Verisign for .com), which is the official record, and shows the age in years, months and days.

Is this domain age checker free?

Yes. It is free with no signup and no daily limit. Check as many domains as you like.

Is website age the same as domain age?

Not always. Domain age counts from the day the name was registered. A website can go live years later, or a domain can be re-registered after an older site on it expired. To see when a site was first online, look up its earliest snapshot on the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine.

Can I paste a full URL or a subdomain?

Yes. Paste a full address such as https://blog.example.com/page and the checker reduces it to the registered domain, example.com, because only the registered domain has a registration date.

Is domain age a Google ranking factor?

Google has said domain age itself is not a ranking factor. Older sites often rank well because they have built up content and links over time, not because of the date.

Why does the age reset after a domain was dropped?

If a domain expires and someone else registers it again, the registry creates a new record with a new creation date. The age shown is that of the current registration.

Why does my country domain show no data?

The tool uses RDAP, the modern replacement for WHOIS. All generic domains (.com, .org, .net and new ones) support it, but some country-code registries do not publish RDAP data yet.
