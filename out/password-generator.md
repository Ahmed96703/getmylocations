## How strong is strong enough?

| Password | Entropy | Offline guessing time\* |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 characters, all types | ≈ 51 bits | about 5 hours |
| 12 characters, all types | ≈ 77 bits | about 300 centuries |
| 15 characters, all types | ≈ 97 bits | longer than the age of the universe |
| 16 characters, all types | ≈ 103 bits | longer than the age of the universe |
| 4 random words | ≈ 52 bits | about 5 hours |
| 6 random words | ≈ 78 bits | about 350 centuries |

\* At 100 billion guesses a second against a fast hash. Online logins allow far fewer guesses, but databases leak, so plan for the offline case.

Switch to **Passphrase** to use it as a **passphrase generator** or **memorable password generator**: a password made of random words, such as a phrase for your computer login or password manager. Five or six words beat a short random password and are much easier to type. For everything saved in a password manager, a long **strong password** of random characters is best.

Need a **PIN generator**? Turn off everything except Numbers and set the length to 6 or more.

## Good password habits

-   **Never reuse a password.** One leaked site then unlocks every other account with the same one.
-   **Use a password manager** to store a different random password for each site.
-   **Turn on two-factor authentication** for email, banking and social accounts.
-   **Change a password** when a service reports a breach, not on a fixed schedule.
-   **Create a strong password** every time, and **don’t build passwords from personal details** like names, birthdays or pets. A password maker with real randomness is far harder to guess.

## Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to use an online password generator?

This one creates passwords inside your browser with its cryptographically secure random generator. Nothing is sent to a server or saved, so no one else ever sees them. You can even disconnect from the internet before pressing New.

How do I generate a 15-character password?

Press 15 under Quick length, or drag the slider to 15. With uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols turned on, a 15-character password has about 97 bits of entropy, far beyond what any attacker can guess.

Is this password generator free?

Yes. It is free with no signup and no limit: press New as often as you like and copy any of the suggestions.

Can it suggest a password for me?

Yes. As soon as the page opens it suggests five strong random passwords. Pick one, copy it, or press New for five more. It works for any site or device, including Wi-Fi routers and apps where your browser does not offer its own suggestion.

How long should a password be?

At least 12 characters for everyday accounts, 16 or more for email, banking and your password manager. Length adds more strength than symbols do.

Password or passphrase?

A passphrase of five random words, like Fable-Orbit-Mango-Cider-Tulip-42, is as strong as a 10-character random password and far easier to type and remember. Use passphrases for things you type by hand, random passwords for everything stored in a password manager.

What does “bits of entropy” mean?

It measures how many guesses an attacker would need: each extra bit doubles them. 60 bits is strong for online accounts; 80 or more is very strong even against offline attacks on stolen password databases.
