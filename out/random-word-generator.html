Ideas for random words

  • Games: Pictionary, charades, Taboo-style guessing and word association.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the words come from?

From the EFF long word list: 7,776 common, easy-to-spell English words chosen by the Electronic Frontier Foundation, published under CC BY 3.0. Obscure and offensive words were left out of it.

Are the words truly random?

Yes. Each word is picked with your browser’s cryptographically secure random generator, with equal odds for every word, and no word repeats within one list.

Can I use these words for a passphrase?

Yes, five or six random words make a strong, memorable passphrase. The password generator has a passphrase mode that also shows how strong it is.
