## Getting an accurate result

-   **Pause other downloads** and streaming on the same connection.
-   **Test close to the router,** or on a cable, to separate Wi-Fi problems from the line itself.
-   **Test at different times:** evenings are often slower when the whole neighborhood is online.
-   **Turn off a VPN,** which adds distance and overhead, unless you want to measure the VPN.

Speeds are in megabits per second; file sizes are in megabytes, eight times larger. A 100 Mbps connection downloads about 12.5 MB each second. Convert any figure with the [bytes and bits converter](https://getmylocations.com/bytes-bits-converter).

## Frequently asked questions

What is a good internet speed?

For one person, 25 Mbps download handles HD streaming and video calls. A household streaming 4K on several screens is comfortable with 100 Mbps or more. For video calls and gaming, ping under 50 ms and jitter under 10 ms matter more than raw speed.

Why is my result lower than my plan?

Plans are sold as “up to” speeds. Wi-Fi distance and walls, other devices using the connection, an old router or a busy time of day all lower the result. For the most accurate figure, test on a cable connection with other devices idle.

What are ping and jitter?

Ping is how long a tiny message takes to reach the server and come back. Jitter is how much that time varies. High jitter causes choppy calls and lag in games even on a fast connection.

Which server does the test use?

Cloudflare’s speed-test endpoints, answered by whichever of its data centers is closest to you, so the result reflects your connection rather than a distant server.

How much data does the test use?

On a fast connection up to about 250 MB, less on slower ones. On a limited mobile plan, run it only when you need to.
