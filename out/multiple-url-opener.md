## How to open many links at once

1.  Paste your list, one link per line. Spaces and commas also work as separators.
2.  Press **Open all**. If only one tab opens, allow pop-ups for this site and press it again.
3.  Or press **Open next** repeatedly to open them one by one; opened links turn grey.

## Handy uses

-   **Checking a batch of pages** after a site update or a redirect change.
-   **Morning routine:** open the same set of dashboards and news sites.
-   **Research:** open every result from a list in one go. To collect all links from a page first, use the [link analyzer](https://getmylocations.com/link-analyzer) and copy them.

Links open with **noopener**, so the opened pages cannot control this tab.

## Frequently asked questions

Why does only one tab open?

Browsers allow one new tab per click to stop pop-up spam. Allow pop-ups for this site once (click the blocked pop-up icon in the address bar) and “Open all” will open the whole list. “Open next” always works without changing any settings.

How many links can I open?

There is no limit in the tool, but each tab uses memory. Opening more than 20 to 30 at once can slow a computer down; open large lists in batches.

Do I need to type https://?

No. Bare domains such as example.com get https:// added. Duplicates and anything that is not a web address are skipped.

Is my list saved?

No. The list stays in your browser tab and is gone when you close or reload the page.
