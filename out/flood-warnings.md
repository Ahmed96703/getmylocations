## Flood warning levels and what to do

| Level | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flood alert | Flooding is possible | Be prepared: check your flood plan, move cars and valuables from low ground, keep up to date |
| Flood warning | Flooding is expected | Act now: move family, pets and valuables upstairs, turn off gas, electricity and water if safe, put flood protection in place |
| Severe flood warning | Danger to life | Follow the emergency services; be ready to leave; call 999 if you are in immediate danger |
| No longer in force | No further flooding expected | Take care: floodwater can still be deep, fast and dirty |

Never walk, drive or swim through floodwater. Thirty centimetres of moving water is enough to float a car, and drains and manholes may be open under the surface.

## Frequently asked questions

Are there flood warnings near me now?

Press "Floods near me" or search a postcode or town in England. The tool lists every flood alert, flood warning and severe flood warning in force within the distance you choose, straight from the Environment Agency, which updates them every 15 minutes.

What is the difference between a flood alert and a flood warning?

A flood alert means flooding is possible, so be prepared. A flood warning means flooding is expected, so act now. A severe flood warning means there is a danger to life. When the risk has passed, the Environment Agency marks it "no longer in force".

How do I read the river levels?

Each gauge is compared with its own normal range, the band the river stays within about 90% of the time. "Above normal range" means the river is higher than usual there, which does not by itself mean flooding. Levels are measured against each gauge's own reference point, so do not compare numbers between gauges.

Does it cover Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland?

No. Each nation runs its own warnings: Natural Resources Wales, SEPA Floodline in Scotland, and the Department for Infrastructure in Northern Ireland. The tool links to each one.

How can I get flood warnings sent to my phone?

Sign up for free flood warnings on GOV.UK, by phone, text or email, for your address in England, or call Floodline on 0345 988 1188. The service will call you when a warning is issued for your area, even at night.
