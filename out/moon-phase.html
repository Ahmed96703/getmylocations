Moon phase today: moonrise, moonset and the next full moon

See the moon phase right now, how much of it is lit and how old it is, the dates of the next full moon and new moon, and moonrise and moonset times for your location or any place. Includes a monthly moon calendar and works for any date. Free, no signup.

The phases of the moon

PhaseDays after new moonWhen it is up
New moon0Up during the day, next to the sun; not visible
Waxing crescent1 to 6Evening sky in the west after sunset
First quarterabout 7.4Afternoon and first half of the night

Waning crescent23 to 29Early morning sky in the east before sunrise

The moon does not make its own light. Its phase is how much of the sunlit half we can see from Earth as it orbits us once every 29.5 days, the time from one new moon to the next.

Best nights for stargazing and photos

For the darkest skies and the Milky Way, go out in the days around the new moon, when there is no moonlight. For photos of the moon itself, a few days either side of first or last quarter is best, because the low sun angle along the edge of the lit part shows craters in sharp relief. A full moon close to the horizon just after it rises, around sunset, looks large and orange and lights up the landscape; check the moonrise time above and the sunset time for the same place.

Frequently asked questions

What phase is the moon today?

The tool shows it as soon as the page opens: the phase name, the percentage of the moon that is lit, whether it is growing (waxing) or shrinking (waning), and how many days it is since the last new moon. Change the date to see any other day.

When is the next full moon?

The list under the moon gives the dates and times of the next full moons, new moons and quarters, in your time zone or that of the place you search. A full moon happens about every 29.5 days, so most months have one and a few have two.

What time does the moon rise tonight?

Search your town or use your location to get moonrise and moonset for that place. The moon rises about 50 minutes later each day. A full moon rises around sunset, and a new moon rises with the sun, which is why it cannot be seen.

Is the moon phase the same everywhere?

Yes, the phase is the same for everyone on Earth at a given moment, though the date can differ across time zones. What changes is the view: in the Southern Hemisphere the moon appears upside down compared with the north, so a waxing crescent is lit on the left instead of the right.

What is a blue moon or a supermoon?

A blue moon is usually the second full moon in one calendar month, which happens about every two to three years. A supermoon is a full moon close to the moon's nearest point to Earth, when it looks slightly bigger and brighter; the tool shows the moon's distance each day.
