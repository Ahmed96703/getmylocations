What are bits and bytes?

A bit is the smallest piece of digital data: a single 0 or 1. A byte is a group of 8 bits, enough to store one letter of plain English text. Larger units are built from these: a kilobyte, megabyte and gigabyte are a thousand, a million and a billion bytes. Network speeds are counted in bits per second, and file sizes in bytes. Mixing the two up is the most common reason a download seems eight times slower than the speed you pay for.

Real downloads usually take a little longer. Part of every connection carries network overhead rather than your file, Wi-Fi is often slower than the line itself, and a busy server may not send at your full speed. Many browsers, game launchers and download managers show progress in megabytes per second (MB/s), so a steady 12 MB/s on a 100 Mbps line is close to the maximum of 12.5.

Why a new drive shows less space than advertised

Drive makers count in decimal units, where 1 TB is 1012 bytes. Windows counts in binary units (1 GiB = 1,0243 bytes) but labels them “GB”, so the same drive shows a smaller number. Nothing is missing. This is how each size appears in Windows before formatting and system files take their share:

macOS, iOS and Android use decimal units, so there a 1 TB drive shows close to 1 TB.

Speeds and sizes

  • Internet speed is in megabits per second: 100 Mbps = 12.5 megabytes per second.
  • A 4 GB film takes about 5 minutes 20 seconds at 100 Mbps.
  • Phone storage and data plans are in decimal gigabytes.

Measure your own connection with the internet speed test, then plug the result into the download time calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How many MB are in a GB?

1,000 MB in decimal units, the ones used for drives, phones and data plans. In binary units, 1 GiB is 1,024 MiB, which Windows shows as “1 GB = 1,024 MB”.

What is 1 Gbps in MB per second?

125 MB/s. Divide any speed in Mbps by 8 to get MB/s, so a gigabit connection can download up to about 125 megabytes each second at full speed.

How many bits are in a byte?

Eight. Internet speeds are given in bits per second (Mbps) and file sizes in bytes (MB), so divide a speed by 8 to get megabytes per second: 100 Mbps is 12.5 MB/s.

Is 1 GB 1000 MB or 1024 MB?

Both are in use. Drive makers and networking use decimal units (1 GB = 1,000 MB). Windows and memory use binary units (1 GiB = 1,024 MiB) but often label them “GB”. That is why a 1 TB drive shows about 931 GB in Windows.

What is the difference between Mb and MB?

A small b means bits, a capital B means bytes. 1 MB (megabyte) = 8 Mb (megabits).

How long does it take to download 1 GB?

At 100 Mbps, about 1 minute 20 seconds at full speed; at 25 Mbps about 5 minutes 20 seconds; at 1 Gbps about 8 seconds. Use the download time calculator above for any size and speed.
