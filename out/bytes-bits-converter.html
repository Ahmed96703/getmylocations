Speeds and sizes

  • Internet speed is in megabits per second: 100 Mbps = 12.5 megabytes per second.
  • A 4 GB film takes about 5 minutes 20 seconds at 100 Mbps.
  • Phone storage and data plans are in decimal gigabytes.

Measure your own connection with the internet speed test, then plug the result into the download time calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How many bits are in a byte?

Eight. Internet speeds are given in bits per second (Mbps) and file sizes in bytes (MB), so divide a speed by 8 to get megabytes per second: 100 Mbps is 12.5 MB/s.

Is 1 GB 1000 MB or 1024 MB?

Both are in use. Drive makers and networking use decimal units (1 GB = 1,000 MB). Windows and memory use binary units (1 GiB = 1,024 MiB) but often label them “GB”. That is why a 1 TB drive shows about 931 GB in Windows.

What is the difference between Mb and MB?

A small b means bits, a capital B means bytes. 1 MB (megabyte) = 8 Mb (megabits).

How long does it take to download 1 GB?

At 100 Mbps, about 1 minute 20 seconds at full speed; at 25 Mbps about 5 minutes 20 seconds; at 1 Gbps about 8 seconds. Use the download time calculator above for any size and speed.
